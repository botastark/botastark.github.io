About

Salem! I'm a PhD student in the Neuro Computing Systems Lab at KTH and part of the ELEVATE doctoral network, exploring how event-based cameras and spiking neural networks can support fast, low-power robot perception.

Before Stockholm I was a Research Fellow at ISTC-CNR in Rome, working on autonomous drone missions for precision agriculture, including information-gain path planning, Jetson + PX4 integration, and RGB/LiDAR mapping. Earlier roles included document-understanding work at Botshelf.ai, industrial OCR and UR10 motion planning at Baker Hughes, and robotic manipulation research at Nazarbayev University.

I'm happy to talk about event vision, field robotics or collaborations. Get in touch.
