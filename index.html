Salem! I'm an AI & robotics engineer with experience across field robotics, computer vision, and applied machine learning. I'm joining the Neuro Computing Systems Lab at KTH Royal Institute of Technology as a PhD student and MSCA scholar within the ELEVATE consortium — a direction I'm genuinely excited to grow into, focusing on neuromorphic computing and event-based vision.

Most recently I was a Research Fellow at ISTC CNR (Italian National Research Council) in Rome, where I deployed autonomous drones for precision agriculture — working on real-time mapping, mission planning, and sensor fusion with RGB and LiDAR.

Before CNR, I was an AI Engineer at Botshelf.ai, building production document understanding pipelines that combined OCR, layout analysis, and large language models. Earlier, at Nazarbayev University, I worked on robotic manipulation and perception using the UR10 arm and an RGBD camera.

My broader interests are in perception, autonomous systems, and how AI can make robots more capable in unstructured environments. For collaborations or a chat — reach out anytime.
