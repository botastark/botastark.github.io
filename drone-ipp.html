Overview

✦information gain path · field mission

Developed information-gain-based path planning for agricultural UAV mapping, taking the system from real aerial imagery to field deployment at ISTC-CNR.

  • Integrated PX4 and NVIDIA Jetson onboard hardware.
  • Built TCP/UDP services for state feedback, video streaming, and commands.
  • Applied information-gain path planning to real aerial imagery.
  • Presented the work orally at IAS-19.
