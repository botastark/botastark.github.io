Analytics

This website loads Google Analytics only after you choose “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner. Analytics is used to understand aggregated visitor activity.

Your choice is stored in a first-party cookie named bota_analytics_consent for 180 days. You can change it by clearing this site’s cookies in your browser and reloading the page.

Google may process analytics data according to its own Privacy Policy.
